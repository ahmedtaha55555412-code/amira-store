# PHASE 10 — Homepage + Content Pages + Settings-Driven Branding

## Objective
Complete the premium storefront experience and editable content.

## Homepage final sections
1. Announcement bar
2. Header/navigation
3. Hero
4. Five category cards
5. New Arrivals — database-driven by actual `created_at`, newest first
6. Offers — database-driven where variant current price is below original price
7. Benefits
8. Brand story
9. Approved site reviews
10. WhatsApp testimonials
11. WhatsApp CTA
12. Footer

## Hard exclusions
No:
- featured products
- selected products
- best sellers
- algorithmic recommendations
- brand carousel
- coupon block
- mandatory newsletter

## Admin homepage controls
Admin may control:
- text
- images
- hero banners
- section visibility
- section ordering
- CTA labels/links

Admin must NOT select individual products for New Arrivals or Offers; those sections are query-driven.

## Static/content pages
Implement:
- About
- Contact
- Privacy
- Terms
- Shipping
- legal/policy content as required

Do not publish a blanket return/exchange statement that conflicts with applicable law; legal copy must be reviewed for Egypt before launch.

## Brand settings
Admin can replace:
- logo
- favicon
- WhatsApp number
- WhatsApp message template
- social links
- support/contact text
- footer text

## Definition of Done
Homepage looks complete and the content can be maintained without code changes while preserving business rules.
